# Media assets

`MediaAsset` is the foundation for associating images, videos, infographics, and
documents with a post. It records whether material was generated by us, supplied
as an official release, or uploaded directly, together with its origin, license,
location, optional thumbnail, and structured metadata.

## Media policy

- AI-generated images are the default illustration for normal articles.
- Official assets may be used only when they are official releases or official
  materials, such as company press releases, official documents, diagrams, and
  official videos.
- Random third-party article images are never imported or copied.

This phase only stores asset references and exposes attached assets for reading.
It does not generate media, download external files, scrape source images, upload
files, or publish media to Telegram.
