# Media assets

`MediaAsset` is the foundation for associating images, videos, infographics, and
documents with a post. It records whether material was generated by us, supplied
as an official release, or uploaded directly, together with its origin, license,
location, optional thumbnail, and structured metadata.

## Phase 2 decision matrix

| News/material | Verification | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Normal news, with or without an article image | Any | `generate_image` |
| Official release image or diagram | Confirmed | `use_official_image` |
| Official release infographic | Confirmed | `use_official_infographic` |
| Official release PDF/document | Confirmed | `use_official_document` |
| Official video | Confirmed official publisher/channel | `use_official_video` |
| Missing, ambiguous, contradictory, or unverified candidate | Not confirmed | `generate_image` |

Strategies are typed values owned by `MediaResolver`; callers do not assemble
policy strings. The resolver is a pure domain service: it consumes normalized
metadata and performs no network access.

## Official-source criteria

An official candidate requires all of the following:

1. `verified_official_source` is explicitly true;
2. an approved explicit material type is present;
3. URL, origin, license type, and non-empty provenance metadata are present;
4. the URL matches the declared official organization domain **or** the
   publisher/channel identifier matches its declared official identifier; and
5. non-video materials belong to an official release.

A URL alone is never evidence of official status. Origin, source URL, license,
and provenance are copied to the plan so a later stage can audit the decision.
Conflicting evidence fails closed.

## Fallback and article-image policy

The safe fallback for every incomplete, unknown, or unconfirmed input is a
pending AI-generated illustration. Images embedded in ordinary news pages,
third-party images, video reuploads, and unofficial channels are never selected,
scraped, imported, or copied, even when their URL is available.

Planning creates one neutral `MediaAsset` by default. Its metadata contains a
nullable `language_code`; a future stage may request separate `ru` and `en`
generated assets without duplicating every plan now.

## Phase boundaries

Phase 2 resolves policy and stores a preparatory `MediaAsset` reference only.
For generated media, the reference is marked `generation_required`; for official
media, it is marked `reference_ready`. The planning service does not fetch or
validate a URL over the network.

Later phases may implement generation workers, controlled downloads, durable
storage, language-specific variants, and publisher integration. This phase does
not generate images, download media, scrape pages, upload to S3/CDN, or publish
media to Telegram, and it adds no parallel publishing pipeline.
